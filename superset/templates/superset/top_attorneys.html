Ranking Methodology

Our practitioner rankings are calculated using a weighted composite score based on four key performance metrics:

Composite Score Formula:

Score = (35% × Grant Rate) + (25% × Speed) + (25% × Grant Volume) + (15% × Application Volume)

Use the floating controls on the right to customize ranking weights →

Metric Definitions:

  • Grant Rate (35%): Percentage of cases granted (Granted Cases ÷ Total Cases)
  • Speed (25%): Processing efficiency (1 ÷ Average Pendency Days)
  • Grant Volume (25%): Total number of cases granted
  • Application Volume (15%): Total number of cases handled by signing Application Data Sheet (ADS)

Data Processing: All metrics are normalized using min-max scaling (0-1) before applying weights to ensure fair comparison across different value ranges.

Grant Rate Accuracy: Applications still in process are excluded from grant rate calculations to provide more accurate success metrics based only on completed cases.

Data Source & Disclaimer

All data presented in this ranking is sourced from the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) public records. The information includes patent applications meta data and documents. While we strive for accuracy, this data is provided for informational purposes only and should not be the sole factor in selecting legal representation. Individual case outcomes may vary based on numerous factors not reflected in these statistics. Users are encouraged to conduct their own due diligence when selecting patent counsel.

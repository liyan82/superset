🏆 Ranking Methodology

Our comprehensive ranking system evaluates companies using a weighted composite score based on four key performance indicators:

Composite Score = (35% × Grant Rate) + (25% × Speed) + (25% × Grant Volume) + (15% × Application Volume)
  • Grant Rate (35% weight): Percentage of patent applications successfully granted, indicating the company's effectiveness in patent prosecution
  • Speed (25% weight): Inverse of average pendency days, measuring how efficiently the company navigates the patent process
  • Grant Volume (25% weight): Total number of patents successfully granted, demonstrating the company's track record and experience
  • Application Volume (15% weight): Total patent applications filed, indicating the company's market presence and innovation activity

Normalization Process: All metrics are normalized using min-max scaling to ensure fair comparison across different scales, then combined using the weighted formula above.

Data Accuracy: Grant rates exclude applications currently in process to provide accurate success rate calculations based on final outcomes only.

While we strive for accuracy, this data is provided for informational purposes only and should not be the sole factor in making business decisions. Individual case outcomes may vary based on numerous factors not reflected in these statistics. Users are encouraged to conduct their own due diligence when evaluating patent portfolios.
